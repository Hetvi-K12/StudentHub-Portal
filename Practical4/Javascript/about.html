About StudentHub Portal

StudentHub is a student portal designed to provide students with easy access to important academic information and resources in one centralised platform.

The portal allows students to access their profile, study materials, timetable, assignments, and academic results. It provides a simple and user-friendly interface that helps students manage and access their academic information efficiently.

StudentHub aims to make academic information more organised, accessible, and convenient for students by bringing essential student services together in one place.

Key Features

  • Student Dashboard
  • Student Profile
  • Study Materials
  • Time Table
  • Assignment Management
  • Academic Results
  • Contact and Support
